About Songles

Songles celebrates the magic of people singing together.
Whether you're completely new to harmony singing or have sung for years,
Songles is a place to:

Learn simple harmony songs
Explore songs from different traditions
Experience the joy of singing together
Find confidence in your own voice

Why harmony singing?
Harmony singing has a unique way of bringing people together.
When individual voices become one, something profound happens. The music carries a new energy, and the people singing it experience a shared resonance that reaches beyond the song itself.
That shared joy and collective uplift are at the heart of Songles.

Meet Songles

Songles is primarily led by Abhi, a singer, harmony enthusiast and lifelong student of music, born from a desire to share the joy, connection and magic of singing together.

Abhi’s musical journey began as a kid learning Rabindrasangeet (Tagore songs), a beautiful tradition that weaves together Hindustani classical music, Bengali folk traditions and Western melodies.

This early foundation shaped her curiosity, versatility and deep love for exploring different musical traditions.

Years later, discovering harmony singing transformed the way she experienced music. Singing with Hackney Harmony Choir and learning from her choir leader Sophia Zoe brought her immense joy, connection and community, inspiring her to create Songles in 2026 as a way of sharing this gift with others.

While Abhi leads Songles, she is often joined by wonderful musician friends like Dan and Nadia who bring their own unique styles, rhythms and creativity into the experience. Through percussion, drumming and diverse musical influences, these collaborations add new colours and dimensions to the collective journey of harmony singing.

At heart, she is a lifelong student of music, forever curious, exploring new sounds and sharing in the beautiful journey of voices coming together..
